# Vault operating manual

> Copy this file into the root of your vault. Every agent in this pack reads it first to learn
> where things go and how you want them written. Edit it freely — it is yours, and the agents
> follow whatever it says.

## What this vault is

A folder of plain Markdown files holding what this business knows: clients, calls, offers,
decisions, research and the reasoning behind them. Plain text on purpose, so it stays readable
and portable forever, with no company able to lock it away.

## Folders

| Folder | What belongs there |
|---|---|
| `Projects/` | Work with an end date |
| `Areas/` | Things that never end: clients, finance, marketing, operations |
| `People/` | One note per client, prospect, partner or supplier |
| `Meetings/` | Call notes and transcripts, dated |
| `Resources/` | Reference material: documents, books, research |
| `Ideas/` | Sparks and half-thoughts, unsorted |
| `Wiki/` | What you know, written down once |
| `Archive/` | Finished or dead. Never deleted |

Rename them if you prefer other names; just update this table and the agents will follow.

## How notes are written

- Every note opens with frontmatter: `date`, `type`, `tags`, and `ai-first: true`
- Then two or three sentences of context for whoever reads it next, including your AI
- Link people, projects and ideas with `[[double brackets]]` so the vault connects itself
- Record where something came from, and when. A claim without a source ages badly

## Standing rules

1. **Never delete.** Move it to `Archive/` with a line saying why. Things come back.
2. **Capture the reason, not just the decision.** The reasoning is what is reusable later.
3. **Isolate the sensitive.** Finance in `Areas/Finance/`, anything confidential in its own
   area. Never in general `Resources/`.
4. **Same-day capture.** A record that is not current is not a record.
5. **Drafts, not sends.** Anything going to another human is proposed to you first. Nothing
   leaves on its own.
6. **Never reproduce source material.** Ingest so you can think with it. What you publish is
   yours, in your words.
